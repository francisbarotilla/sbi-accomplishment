Para sa mga magulang

Bakuna Eskwela key messages

Ligtas at epektibo

Vaccines given in schools were rigorously studied and found safe and effective by experts.

Delikado ang mga sakit na ito

Measles, rubella, tetanus, diphtheria and HPV can cause serious complications, cervical cancer or even death.

Libre sa paaralan

Vaccines are FREE in public schools during the campaign. Please sign the consent form so your child can be vaccinated.

May tanong?

Ask your child's teacher, or visit your Barangay Health Station or the Pangantucan Rural Health Unit.

Source: Pangantucan RHU, SBI Recording Forms 1–3. Percent = vaccinated ÷ eligible learners in the 2026 SBI microplan.Totals only — no learner names or personal data are published (RA 10173).
